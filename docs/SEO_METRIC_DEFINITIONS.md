# SEO metric definitions

- Clicks and impressions are reported by Google Search Console.
- CTR is clicks divided by impressions when impressions are non-zero.
- Average position means “Average position reported by Google Search Console”; it is not a guaranteed fixed ranking.
- GSC and GA4 use different reporting and attribution definitions; this workflow does not claim direct causation between their metrics.
- Opportunities are deterministic review signals and do not prove why traffic or ranking changed.
